# What we changed, and why

Receipts for the "what we changed" slide: tester quote → change → commit → measured effect. Quotes are verbatim from `sense_checks.md` (Sense Check 1, test 6e6a9082), except row 9, which comes from the call transcript as logged in `feedback_changes.md`. Details and owners are in `feedback_changes.md`. Numbers and their caveats are in `slide_numbers.md`.

**Read the "How it relates" column before you write a slide line.** It uses exactly three labels:
- **changed after the tester:** can be pitched as "we changed it because a tester said…".
- **built before the call, tester asked for it:** can only be pitched as "a tester asked for exactly this, and it was already there".
- **our own testing:** is not tester feedback at all.

Caveat on the testers (from `feedback_changes.md`): neither is our exact target user. Tester A works in compliance (role not stated). Tester B was on the regulator side.
