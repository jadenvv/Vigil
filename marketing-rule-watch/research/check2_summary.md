# Sense Check 2 summary

**Check 2: "why marketing issues slip through, and who needs this most"** (test `bf380ee1`, interview, stimulus https://marketing-rule-watch-a0747dd2.base44.app/demo).

**Status when pulled (~17:3x, 10-10): in progress, 1 of 2 good calls in.** More may arrive. Pulled read-only by p4-eval with `get_results`. Quotes are verbatim from the call transcript.

## Five points (Tester 1)

1. **No recent hands-on review.** The tester hasn't reviewed website or social posts for compliance "in years". They described today's practice only generally: people go through the posts and make judgment calls. They gave no real examples of misses.
2. **What they liked in the demo:** a clean interface, a clear headline per issue, the exact snippet, and the confidence percentage, which they read as a way to judge how far to trust the tool. In their words: "It's just super easy to to to kinda fly through these."
3. **Why they'd want it:** it checks advisers' pages and other sites, not just the main site, and it runs in the background. People reviewing by hand take shortcuts and lose the overall view. In their words: "it's easy to do shortcuts. And so when that happens, you're probably missing such a a global view like this is this is doing through the the looking that it does."
4. **Dislikes: none.** But they only saw the overview: "obviously, I can't go beyond this page". So this is no evidence yet about trust in a single flag's detail. Check 3's open-a-flag task targets exactly that gap.
5. **AI in finance:** they have "no issue with it at all", with a condition: "It's something that you you have to keep an eye on and certainly have to double check work". That echoes Check 1's human-in-the-loop point.

## Caveats

- One tester, and the check is still in progress.
- "I would assume it's gonna catch more than a person would" is an **assumption, not experience**. Don't present it as evidence that the tool catches more.
- The tester hasn't done this review in years, so this is weak evidence for H1 (risk lives outside the review workflow) and H3 (officers won't trust a flag without quote plus rule).
- The tester commented on the demo overview only, not on a flag's evidence view, the rewrite or the approval loop.
