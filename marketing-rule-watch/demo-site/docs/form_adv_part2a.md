## Item 4 – Advisory Business

Quarrywick Wealth was founded in 2011 and is wholly owned by Odile Ketteridge, CFP®, who serves as the Firm's founder, lead advisor and Chief Compliance Officer. Tomas Wrenfield serves as an associate advisor.

We provide financial planning and investment management services to individuals, families, trusts and small business owners. As of December 31, 2025, we served approximately 180 households.

Financial planning services may include cash flow analysis, retirement projections, Social Security timing, education funding and estate document review. Plans are updated at least once a year for ongoing clients.

Investment management services consist of diversified portfolios built primarily from index mutual funds and exchange-traded funds across taxable and retirement accounts. We manage most accounts to one of three model strategies: Core Growth, Balanced Income and Income Shield. Clients may impose reasonable restrictions on investing in certain securities or types of securities.

For clients within five years of retirement, we offer the Income Shield retirement program, which sets aside a reserve of conservative holdings (generally short-term bond funds and money market funds) intended to fund one to three years of planned withdrawals. Income Shield is an asset allocation approach within the client's advisory account. It is described further in Item 8.

We do not participate in a wrap fee program.

As of December 31, 2025, Quarrywick Wealth managed $214,600,000 in client assets on a discretionary basis and $8,900,000 on a non-discretionary basis, for total regulatory assets under management of $223,500,000.

## Item 5 – Fees and Compensation

### Investment management fees

We charge an annual advisory fee based on the value of assets under management, according to the following tiered schedule:

| Assets under management | Annual fee |
|---|---|
| First $1,000,000 | 1.00% |
| Next $2,000,000 ($1,000,001 to $3,000,000) | 0.75% |
| Next $2,000,000 ($3,000,001 to $5,000,000) | 0.60% |
| Above $5,000,000 | 0.45% |

Fees are tiered, which means each rate applies only to the assets within that tier. For example, a client with $1,500,000 under management would pay $13,750 per year ($10,000 on the first $1,000,000 plus $3,750 on the next $500,000).

Fees are billed quarterly in advance based on the account value on the last business day of the prior quarter. With the client's written authorization, fees are deducted directly from client accounts by the custodian. Fees for partial quarters are prorated. Either party may terminate the advisory agreement at any time by written notice, and any prepaid, unearned fees will be refunded.

Fees may be negotiable in some circumstances, including for related accounts, legacy clients and employees. As a result, some clients may pay more or less than others for similar services.

### Financial planning fees

Stand-alone financial plans are $3,500 for an initial plan and $1,500 per year for annual updates. Half of the initial planning fee is payable when the engagement begins and the balance on delivery of the plan.

### Other fees and expenses

Clients also pay the internal expenses of the mutual funds and exchange-traded funds held in their accounts (typically 0.05% to 0.30% per year) and any custodian charges, such as transaction fees and wire fees. These costs are in addition to our advisory fee. See Item 12 for a description of our brokerage practices and Item 14 for revenue-sharing arrangements related to certain mutual funds.

### Compensation for the sale of securities or other investment products

Quarrywick Wealth does not receive commissions on securities. However, our supervised persons, acting in their separate capacity as licensed insurance agents of our affiliate, may receive commissions for the sale of insurance and annuity products. See Item 10.

## Item 8 – Methods of Analysis, Investment Strategies and Risk of Loss

### Methods of analysis

We use fundamental analysis of asset classes and funds, long-term capital market assumptions, and periodic review of fund costs and tracking. A small research sleeve in some Core Growth accounts (generally no more than 5% of the account) may hold individual stocks selected using fundamental company analysis.

### Investment strategies

Our strategies emphasize broad diversification, low costs and periodic rebalancing. We do not attempt to time the market. We do not use leverage or short selling.

### Risk of loss

Investing in securities involves risk of loss that clients should be prepared to bear. No investment strategy, including those described in this brochure, can ensure a profit or protect against loss in a declining market.

Market risk. The value of the securities in a client's account will rise and fall with general market conditions, and clients may lose money, including in years when broad markets decline. In calendar year 2022, the Core Growth composite returned -12.4% net of fees (-11.5% gross of fees), and most client accounts declined in value during that year.

Benchmark risk. Our strategies do not track any index and may perform differently from their benchmarks. The Core Growth composite has underperformed its benchmark, net of fees, in some periods, including calendar years 2019, 2021 and 2025. There is no assurance that any of our strategies will outperform its benchmark or any other measure of market performance in the future.

Income Shield risk. Income Shield is not an insurance product and does not guarantee income for any period of time, including for life. The conservative reserve is invested in short-term bond funds and money market funds, and the remainder of the account is invested in diversified portfolios that are subject to market risk. The value of an Income Shield account can decline, and clients participating in Income Shield can lose money. Clients may outlive the assets in their accounts.

Interest rate risk. Bond funds, including those used in the Income Shield reserve, generally lose value when interest rates rise.

Concentration risk. Positions in the research sleeve are less diversified than the rest of the portfolio and may lose a substantial portion of their value.

Cybersecurity and operational risk. We and our service providers rely on information systems that may be subject to failures or breaches.

## Item 10 – Other Financial Industry Activities and Affiliations

Odile Ketteridge owns Quarrywick Insurance Services, LLC, a licensed insurance agency under common control with Quarrywick Wealth. Odile Ketteridge and Tomas Wrenfield are licensed insurance agents of Quarrywick Insurance Services, LLC.

Quarrywick Insurance Services, LLC receives commissions on fixed and fixed indexed annuities and life insurance policies that our advisory clients purchase through it, including clients participating in the Income Shield program.

These commissions are in addition to our advisory fees and give our supervised persons an incentive to recommend insurance products based on the compensation received rather than on a client's needs. This is a conflict of interest. Clients are under no obligation to purchase insurance products through our affiliate and may purchase them from any insurance agent. Assets held in annuities purchased through our affiliate are not included in the advisory fee calculation.

Neither the Firm nor its management persons is registered as a broker-dealer, futures commission merchant, commodity pool operator or commodity trading advisor.

## Item 11 – Code of Ethics, Participation or Interest in Client Transactions and Personal Trading

We have adopted a code of ethics that requires our supervised persons to act in clients' best interests, to comply with applicable securities laws, and to report personal securities holdings and transactions. A copy of the code is available on request.

Our supervised persons may buy or sell for their own accounts the same securities that we recommend to clients, including stocks held in the research sleeve. To address this conflict, personal trades in individual stocks held in client accounts must be pre-cleared by the Chief Compliance Officer and may not be placed ahead of client orders in the same security on the same day.

## Item 12 – Brokerage Practices

We recommend that clients hold their accounts at Fernhollow Custody Services ("Fernhollow"), an unaffiliated, SEC-registered broker-dealer and qualified custodian. We consider Fernhollow's execution quality, custody services, technology, costs and reputation.

Fernhollow provides us with research, software and operational support at no charge. These benefits are not tied to the trades of any particular client, and they create an incentive for us to recommend Fernhollow based on our interest in receiving them rather than on clients' interest in the most favorable execution. We do not have formal soft dollar arrangements.

We may aggregate trades for multiple clients. Clients participating in an aggregated order receive the average price.

## Item 13 – Review of Accounts

Odile Ketteridge or Tomas Wrenfield reviews each investment management account at least quarterly for consistency with the client's objectives and target allocation. Financial plans are reviewed at least annually. Clients receive account statements at least quarterly directly from the custodian, and written performance reports from us each quarter.

## Item 14 – Client Referrals and Other Compensation

Under a revenue-sharing agreement, Quarrywick Wealth receives a portion of the shareholder servicing fees that Fernhollow collects from certain no-transaction-fee mutual funds held in client accounts. These payments are based on the amount of client assets invested in participating funds.

This arrangement gives us an incentive to recommend participating funds over other funds that may have lower internal expenses. We address this conflict by disclosing it here and by reviewing fund selections at least annually for cost and suitability.

As described in Item 10, our supervised persons may also receive insurance commissions through our affiliate.

We do not pay any person for client referrals.

## Item 15 – Custody

We are deemed to have custody of client funds because we have authority to deduct advisory fees directly from client accounts. Clients receive statements at least quarterly from their qualified custodian and should compare them carefully with any reports they receive from us.

## Item 16 – Investment Discretion

Most clients grant us discretionary authority, through the advisory agreement, to select the securities and amounts to be bought or sold without obtaining specific consent for each transaction, subject to any written restrictions the client imposes.

## Item 17 – Voting Client Securities

## Item 18 – Financial Information
