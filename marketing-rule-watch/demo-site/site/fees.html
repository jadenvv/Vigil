Fees

We are fee-only. We do not earn commissions on any product we recommend.

Fees are tiered, billed quarterly in advance based on the account value on the last business day of the prior quarter, and deducted directly from client accounts. A client with $1,500,000 under management would pay $13,750 per year ($10,000 on the first $1,000,000 plus $3,750 on the next $500,000).

Financial planning only

Stand-alone financial plans are $3,500 for an initial plan and $1,500 per year for annual updates.

Other costs

Clients also pay the internal expenses of the mutual funds and exchange-traded funds held in their accounts (typically 0.05% to 0.30% per year) and any custodian charges. We do not share in any of these fees.

Fees may be negotiable in some circumstances. Full details are in our Form ADV Part 2A brochure, available free on request.
